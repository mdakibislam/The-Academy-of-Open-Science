# The Academy of Open Science (AOS)

> **Deconstructing complex science and mathematics into first-principles derivations, intuitive mechanics, and open educational resources.**

The Academy of Open Science (AOS) is an open-source, community-driven academic platform dedicated to making high-level scientific knowledge universally accessible, rigorous, and transparent.

Rather than providing raw formulas to memorize, AOS focuses on **how science actually works** breaking down fundamental equations, step-by-step mathematical proofs, physical concepts, and real-world applications from the ground up.

---

## Core Pillars

* **First-Principles Derivations:** We don't just state formulas; we derive them step-by-step so learners understand the underlying logic.
* **Open & Accessible:** Free forever, zero paywalls, zero ads, and built entirely using standard Markdown and open web standards.
* **Peer-Driven Collaboration:** Built by students, self-taught learners, and educators sharing knowledge through open peer reviews and contributions.
